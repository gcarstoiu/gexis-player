# Phase 30: room correction for the full range

Brief: `design/briefs/30-room-correction-full.md` (docs branch), drawn on what
is built (`design/IMPLEMENTED-DIFFERENTLY.md`, *Room correction*): three
positions, the *Both* tab, the calibration file given from the phone, the
check that waits for a tap, the level at about 60 dB, the chart's reach
growing in 5 dB steps.

Open `Room Correction Board.dc.html` over HTTP from `design/`
(`/source/30/Room%20Correction%20Board.dc.html`); tabs switch theme
(`?theme=bauhaus|sage|velvet|ink|gauge`, none = default). The turn-1 options
are in `Room Correction 30 Options.dc.html`.

## Picks (George, turn 1)

- **1a, how far it reaches:** a *Reaches* label and two chips under on/off,
  in the switch's card on the phone; a card of its own at the top of the
  panel's column; a row of two at the top of the bar's middle column.
  *Bass · Up to 250 Hz*, *Full range · 15 Hz to 20 kHz*. A tap is heard at
  once, so the two compare by the choice itself. Off or DSD: at 0.45, inert.
  A measurement from before this phase: Bass selected, Full range dashed
  (1.5 px at 0.28) with *Measure again* in warn; a tap opens Before (panel
  and bars: the phone sheet). The note under it: *This measurement covers the
  bass only. Measure again to reach the full range.*
- **1d, the chart:** one log axis 15 Hz to 20 kHz broken at 250 Hz into two
  panes, 8 px apart (12 on the panel and bars), each with its own scale named
  over it in mono caps: *Bass ±20 dB* (±15, growing in 5 dB steps for either
  speaker, as built) and *Above the bass ±5 dB* (gridlines ±3). The panes sit
  on `rgba(13,21,28,0.34)`, radius 8. Lines as built; the fills: cut
  `rgba(242,164,143,0.2)`, raised `rgba(126,214,188,0.2)`. One dotted
  target: the bass rise joined at 250 Hz to the speaker's own slope. With
  Bass chosen the upper pane is hatched with *Left alone: Bass is chosen*;
  with a bass-only measurement, *Measured to 250 Hz only* and no curves.
  *Both* before the check draws before, positions and target only.
- **1g, the corrections:** two groups under *Left speaker · 8 corrections*:
  *In the bass · narrow cuts* and *Above the bass · broad, up or down*, two
  columns of frequency and gain, cuts in the artist coral, raises in the
  accent. An empty group says why (*Nothing to cut: near the target
  already.* / *Nothing to correct: within 1.5 dB of the speaker's own
  balance already.* / *Left alone: Bass is chosen.* / *Not measured: this
  measurement covers the bass only.*). *Both* says *Each speaker's
  corrections are on its own tab.*
- **1l, close to the music:** the drawer's Room row is one control:
  *Without · Bass · Full range* (Without in warn, the reach in the accent).
  Bass-only: Full range at 0.4. Off: *Open ›*. DSD: at 0.4. The tray's pill
  and the phone's 56 px button stay one tap, On ↔ Without, and name the
  reach: *Room · Full range* / *Room · Bass* / *Room · Without*; *Full* /
  *Bass* / *Without* on the phone, the glyph alone under 380 px. Never
  measured: no Room control.

## New words (to confirm)

- Before: *Correct what your room adds to the sound*; *The player plays
  sweeps through your speakers and listens with a measurement microphone at
  your seat. It cuts the peaks your room adds to the bass, and above the bass
  evens out the room's broad bumps and dips by 3 dB at most, so your speakers
  keep their own balance.*
- Place: *…keep the room quiet throughout: the sweeps rise from a low rumble
  to a high whistle.* Measuring: *Each sweep ends in a high whistle: keep
  quiet until it stops.* Panel: *Please keep quiet throughout* · *The sweeps
  rise from a low rumble to a high whistle.*
- Result: *Peaks down by up to 14 dB* / *Above the bass: within 1 dB of the
  speaker's own balance.* · *Above the bass: already within 1.5 dB of the
  speaker's own balance, so left as it is.* · *Nothing to cut in the bass* ·
  *Nothing to correct* · *Above the bass: left alone, as Bass is chosen.*
- The correction's heading counts both kinds: *Four cuts in the bass, four
  broad corrections above*; *…, nothing to correct above*; *Nothing to cut in
  the bass, …*.
- Calibration refusals: *This is not a calibration file* · *This is the 0°
  file* / *Choose the 90° one: the microphone points at the ceiling.* · *No
  serial in this file*.
- Hear it without: *Compare: no correction at all, heard at once. Stays
  where you put it.* / *Now without: no correction at all. Switch back to
  hear it.*
