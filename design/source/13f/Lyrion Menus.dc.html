Lyrion’s own menus
My Music, Favourites and the apps, drawn in the panel’s own system for the Standard family (1280 × 800, holding from 711 to 853 tall) and the Bar family (1280 × 400, and 1480 × 320 drawn at its logical 1850 × 400 and shown at 0.8). One rule carries every level: what an entry opens decides its shape. Branches get a glyph in a tinted disc, as Radio does; covers get a grid; tracks and stations are leaf rows that reveal Play · Play next · Add on the first tap.
Sources: Lyrion Standard.dc.html (prop screen), Lyrion Bar.dc.html (props screen, w), glyphs.js (the 46 CSS glyphs), lyrion-data.js (shape and tint for every entry, and the keyword table for app levels). The current-pass screenshots did not reach me; this works from the brief and the repo.
