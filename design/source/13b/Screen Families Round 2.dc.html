Bars · section 1
One rule for each number
Forecast days
days = floor((W − 1112) / 136), 0 to 3
None below 1248 (aspect 3 shows today only), one from 1248, two from 1384, three from 1520. Three is the most, as the Forecast setting says; round 1’s four at 1850 is gone. Both bar files use this rule and nothing else.
Idle, how it adds up
48 padding and 24 drift each side; clock 523; separator with its gaps 81; today 360 (icon 100, gap 20, condition at most 240 on two lines); then 40, and 100 + 36 per day. The clock group drifts sideways by ±24, inside that room. A long condition such as “Thunderstorm, hail” wraps to a second line instead of widening the group.
Queue rows
Clear and the 66 px Playing from row are back, as on the panel’s rail. That leaves three rows of 60 and part of a fourth under the fade, and the list scrolls. Round 1’s five rows were wrong.
Targets under 44 px
The tray’s volume glyph is now a 60 px mute button. The slider knob is 44, and the slider takes a touch anywhere across the tray’s height. The handle’s drag zone is the full-width 44 px band at the strip’s top edge; on the open tray, its bottom 44 px.

Corrections · section 2
The Setup Screen step
Recognition now only suggests: “Is this your screen?”, “What the screen reports matches this model”, and the suggestion is always a tested model. Choose another opens the whole list, by maker, with no reported block and no filter. In the uncertain state nothing is hidden: models matching the reported resolution come first under their own heading, then every maker. Every model carries Tested or Untested; choosing an untested one says so. Choosing any model says the player will restart on it and ask Keep this screen?. Headless shows its warning when chosen. Corrections · section 4
Settings
In Settings.dc.html, as the brief names them. System opens with an Updates group: Release, Check now, Update now (confirmed), Updates (Manual or Automatic) and Update channel (Stable or Testing, with a warning on Testing); the rest is now headed Maintenance. Plugins ends with Upload a plugin, with the panel’s own note and warning; it is hidden when Settings is on the panel, since a file is picked on a phone or a computer.
