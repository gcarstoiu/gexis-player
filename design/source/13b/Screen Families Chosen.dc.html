Family 1 · aspect 1.5 to 1.8
Standard: today’s screens, the hero absorbs the height
Every screen is laid out at a logical width of 1280 and scaled to the screen’s width (0.625 on 800 × 480, 1.5 on 1920 × 1080). The logical height runs from 711 (aspect 1.8) to 853 (1.5); 16:9, the 13.3″, is 720, and the 7″ 800 × 480 is 768. On each screen one element, the hero, takes the difference; margins, gaps, type and every control keep their 800 values.
What absorbs, per screen
  • Now Playing: the artwork, height − 300 (411 / 500 / 553); the meta column takes the width it frees.
  • Library home: cards and New Music tiles scale together, k = (H − 424) / 376 (0.76 / 1 / 1.14).
  • Artist page, album: the photo and the cover, × H / 800.
  • Browse, Artists, Playlists, Radio, queue, Settings: the list shows more or fewer rows.
  • Idle, transition, pairing, volume drawer: nothing; each is centred at its own size.
Type
Uniform: type scales with the screen, 1.5× on 1920 × 1080 and 0.625× on 800 × 480. Nothing is capped. The smallest type is the 11 and 12 px mono labels, about forty of them, which are 0.85 and 0.9 mm tall on the 7″. Corrected in round 2.
Touch floor: 44 px
No target is under 44 logical px and none grows (ADR-0109 decision 4). 44 px is 5.2 mm on the 7″ 800 × 480, 7.5 mm on 1280 × 800 and 10 mm on the 13.3″. Below 44 in the study: row actions (40 px), which take a 44 px hit area around the same glyph, and the Artists alphabet (about 21 px a key), which is a strip you drag along, not keys you tap. Corrected in round 2.

Family 2 · aspect 3 to 5, height 320 to 400
Bar: a reduced panel of strips
Every bar is laid out at a logical height of 400; its logical width is aspect × 400 (1280 on 1280 × 400, 1850 on 1480 × 320, shown at 0.8). Everything keeps its size; one region per strip takes the width, named with each screen.
Touch
44 px is 6.6 mm on the 7.9″ 1280 × 400 and 7.0 mm on the 11.9″ 1480 × 320. Every bar target is 44 or more (round 2).
Type
The standard scale, with display sizes stepped down to fit 400: titles 42 (strip) and 64 (transition, joining), artist 28, album 24, mono times 16, labels 13. Setup keeps its two-metre intent: 40–64 titles, 44 mono values, a 300 px code.
Bar · Now Playing
The strip and its pull-down tray
Artwork, title, artist, album and year, the source mark and a lyrics switch; a full-width progress bar; shuffle, previous, play, next and repeat; the queue. The handle under the accent line pulls down a tray with Home, the visualiser and volume; it goes back up by dragging or a tap on the strip. Width goes to the progress bar and the tray’s slider.

Bar · Browse
Library home and what its tiles open to
The panel’s six tiles, scaled, beside a 124 px rail on the right: cover, the renderer’s mark, play, and the progress filling up its inner edge. The home strip (New Music, Most played or Recently played) is a tile. Every library screen shares the frame: a 220 px column with Back (and Home two levels in), the title and count; the content; the rail. The artist page and album drop the column for a full-height picture. Width goes to the tiles, panes and rows.

Both families
The visualiser’s surround
In: the visualiser button (in the tray on a bar) or five minutes of playback nobody touches. On screen: the skin and nothing else. Out: a touch anywhere, or a new source, returns to Now Playing; playback stopping keeps the skin five minutes, then idle (ADR-0019, ADR-0036). A screen uses the skins drawn for its exact size; with none, the largest set that fits, centred at 1:1, with opaque black around it, as letterbox.py does for the 720 packs today. The picker offers only that set.

Phone and Settings
Knowing which screen is attached
Setup’s "Is a screen attached?" becomes a Screen step: the model the player recognised, with This is right / Choose another; what the screen reported and a list to choose from; or, with nothing on the screen yet, the list and a warning that the player restarts and tries it. Headless stays the last choice on each. Settings → Display gains Attached screen and Screen rotation, both restarting the player (confirmed).

What small and bar screens leave out
Dropped, and where it is instead
Standard family
Nothing. Rule 1b (one hero per screen absorbs the height, everything else keeps its 800 size) keeps every part at every height; 800 × 480 shows the same screens at 0.625.
Bar: Now Playing
  • The Track / Lyrics / Artist / Release tabs. Lyrics stay, as a switch beside the source mark.
  • Artist info and release info. Not on the bar; the phone does not carry them either, so they are on the standard panel only.
  • The home, visualiser and volume buttons move into the tray.
Bar: Browse
  • The artist page’s biography, top tracks and similar artists.
  • The mini player’s title and artist (the rail shows the cover and the mark).
  • Creating a playlist stays off the device, as everywhere.
Bar: moments
  • Setup’s second QR code (to open the page): the address is text instead.
  • Idle forecast: one day at 1280 × 400.
  • Nothing from transition or pairing.
