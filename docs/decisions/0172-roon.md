# ADR-0172 — Roon

**Status:** **Accepted** 2026-11-01 (George: decisions 1-4), Phase 26
(`ph-roon`). George asked for a plugin for Roon, right after themes
(2026-11-01); the rest is Claude's proposal.

## Context

**Roon plays to devices of its own kinds**: Roon Ready (certified, in the
device's firmware), Roon Bridge (Roon Labs' free endpoint software, RAAT),
Squeezebox, AirPlay, Chromecast and others. The player is none of the first
three today.

**AirPlay already works** (ADR-0167, *Roon*, 2026-11-01): Roon's AirPlay 2
asks for NTP timing, which Shairport Sync refuses; Roon falls back to classic
AirPlay at 44.1 kHz, 16 bits, at once with Roon's *Compatibility mode*. Not
bit-perfect, no more than 44.1 kHz, and Roon's own grouping and DSP stop at
the AirPlay hop.

Found on 2026-11-01 (sources in *Sources*):
- **Roon Bridge** runs on arm64 (`RoonBridge_linuxarmv8.tar.bz2`, Roon's
  installer `roonbridge-installer-linuxarmv8.sh`); it needs glibc 2.34 or
  later, ALSA, and libicu since its .NET 10 build of 31 Aug 2026 (trixie has
  glibc 2.41 and libicu76). It updates itself inside its own directory, so
  that directory is writable by the user it runs as. DietPi runs it as a user
  of its own in `audio`, not as root.
- **It may not be shipped.** Roon's terms forbid making Roon software
  available in any form; DietPi, RoPieee, Volumio (on other hardware) and
  moOde have the user's player download it from Roon Labs, and none of them
  ships it. ADR-0100's pattern: fetched on the device when switched on.
- **It plays to `hw:` devices only** (Roon staff, 2021); a named PCM such as
  `pcm.output` is not offered. An `snd-aloop` loopback card is a `hw:` device
  and is listed. It holds the device while a zone plays and lets it go on
  stop; a busy device is reported as busy in Roon.
- **It shows nothing and offers no control on the device.** What plays, its
  cover and progress, and play, pause, next and volume, come from Roon's
  extension API (`node-roon-api`, Apache-2.0; `roonapi` in Python, Apache-2.0,
  last release 0.1.6 of December 2023), run anywhere on the network once the
  user enables the extension in Roon's *Settings → Extensions*.
- **Roon Ready** is a licence and a certification for a product, at no cost
  but weeks of Roon's testing; no policy for an open-source distribution was
  found. It would give the device's own volume, transport and metadata.
- **Roon's Squeezebox support** serves Squeezebox players from Roon instead of
  Lyrion (one server at a time), is unsupported for Squeezelite, and cannot be
  grouped with Roon's own zones.

## Proposed (Claude)

1. **Roon Bridge, as a plugin like Plexamp** (ADR-0090, ADR-0107): our
   `gexis-roon` package carries the unit, the manifest and an adapter, and
   Roon Bridge itself is fetched from Roon Labs when the plugin is switched on
   (ADR-0100). Off until switched on; switching it on is the consent, with a
   notice that it is Roon Labs' software under Roon's terms.
2. **Roon's own updates.** ADR-0100 pins a URL and a checksum; Roon Bridge
   replaces itself, and an old one may stop working with a newer Roon. The pin
   fetches it once, and Roon's updater keeps it current after.
3. **A user of its own**, `roonbridge`, in `audio`, with the system
   read-only and its own directory writable, as DietPi runs it; not root.
4. **Through the player's output, by a loopback.** A third `snd-aloop` card,
   `Roon`, is the only device Roon Bridge sees; the adapter copies what
   arrives on its capture side into `pcm.output` at the rate it arrives - the
   equaliser, a group and the takeover work as for every source, and with the
   equaliser off nothing is resampled or changed. Roon Bridge playing straight
   to the DAC would bypass all three.
5. **The takeover**: when audio arrives on the loopback the adapter asks the
   core for the device (ADR-0091); asked to let go, it pauses the Roon zone
   through the extension API, then stops copying.
6. **Now Playing through Roon's extension API**, run by the adapter: title,
   artist, album, cover, progress, and play, pause, next, previous and volume
   from the player's screen and phone. It needs the extension enabled once in
   Roon; without it the player shows that Roon plays, nothing more.
7. **Not in this phase**: Roon Ready (asked of Roon Labs later, if at all),
   Squeezebox support, and Roon's own grouping beyond what the player's groups
   do.

## Decided (George)

- **Decision 1, Roon Bridge as a plugin** (2026-11-01), as (1) proposes,
  over AirPlay alone or asking Roon Labs about Roon Ready first.
- **Decision 2, Roon's own updates** (2026-11-01), as (2) proposes: fetched
  once, pinned and checked, then kept current by Roon Bridge itself, under
  its own user (3).
- **Decision 3, through the player's output** (2026-11-01), as (4) proposes:
  a loopback card copied into `pcm.output` at the rate it arrives, so the
  equaliser, groups and the takeover apply, untouched with the equaliser off.
- **Decision 4, Now Playing through Roon's extension** (2026-11-01), as (6)
  proposes: enabled once in Roon; the plugin's page and the manual say so.

## Settings (ADR-0022)

The plugin's switch (*Settings → Plugins*), as every plugin's. No other row
proposed.

## Measured before it is called done

- Roon Bridge fetched and started on a test player; Roon lists it; it plays
  through the loopback to the DAC, bit-perfect with the equaliser off (a test
  signal compared sample by sample), at 44.1 to 192 kHz.
- Takeover both ways with Lyrion and AirPlay; a group plays Roon on every
  member.
- Now Playing and transport from the player with the extension enabled; the
  player says only *Roon* without it.
- Roon Bridge updating itself under its own user, and the plugin switched
  off and on again.
